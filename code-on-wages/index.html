The Code on Wages, 2019

An Act to amend and consolidate the laws relating to wages and bonus and matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

  • Act No. 29 of 2019
  • Assented 8th August, 2019
  • Gazette Gazette of India Extraordinary Part II Sec. 1, No. 48
  • 9 chapters
  • 69 sections

Commencement Partly in force

67 of 69 sections are fully in force; 2 partly in force (s. 42, 67). Each section page shows the notification wording that applies to it.
