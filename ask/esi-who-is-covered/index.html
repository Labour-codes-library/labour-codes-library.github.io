Which workplaces come under ESI?

  • Every establishment with ten or more persons, other than a seasonal factory First Schedule.
  • Every employee in a covered establishment must be insured s. 28(1).

  • “At present, Rs 21,000 per month wages notified for ESI coverage will be applicable.” FAQ 12, Additional FAQs, 16 Mar 2026: official copy · our copy
