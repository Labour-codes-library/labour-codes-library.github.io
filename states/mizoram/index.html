Mizoram: rules under the Labour Codes

These rules apply where the State Government is the appropriate Government; the Central Rules cover establishments for which the Central Government is. Checked on 1 Nov 2026, on official sites only.

  • English
  • official text

Read the rules in full

Official copy, 734 KB · our copy

Also: The pre-published draft, on the department page: official copy

The notification and the heading call these The Mizoram Code on Social Security Rules, 2026; rule 1 gives the short title used here. Rule 1(3) brings these rules into force on a date the State Government appoints by notification. No such notification was found in the e-Gazette up to 1 November 2026. Made in supersession of the Mizoram Code on Social Security Rules, 2023.
