Notices · Industrial Relations Code

Standing orders: certification or adoption

  • Starting the establishment
  • Employer

What to do

Prepare draft standing orders based on the model standing orders, consult the Trade Unions or negotiating union or council, and send the draft to the certifying officer for certification s. 30(1)–(2). If you adopt the model standing orders instead, inform the certifying officer of the date from which they are adopted rule 10.

Who it applies to

Every industrial establishment in which three hundred or more than three hundred workers are employed, or were employed on any day of the preceding twelve months s. 28(1).

In the checklist: Standing orders.

When

The Code required the draft within six months from its commencement s. 30(1); until standing orders are certified, the model standing orders are deemed adopted s. 29(2).

How

  • Send the draft with a statement of the establishment’s details and the strength and details of the workers employed, including the Trade Unions they belong to rule 13(1).

  • If the certifying officer makes no observation within thirty days of being told that the model standing orders have been adopted, they are deemed certified rule 10.
