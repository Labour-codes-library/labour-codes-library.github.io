The Industrial Relations Code, 2020

Section 48. Disqualifications for members of Tribunal and National Industrial Tribunal

In force since 21 Nov 2025Chapter VII — Mechanism for Resolution of Industrial Disputes

Open the definition in the Code

Official text

No person shall be appointed to, or continue in, the office of the member of a Tribunal or National Industrial Tribunal, respectively, if— (a) he is not an independent person; or

(b) he has attained the age of sixty-five years. Explanation.—For the purposes of this section "independent person" means a person who is unconnected with the industrial dispute referred to a Tribunal or National Industrial Tribunal or with any industry directly affected by such dispute.

Source: The Industrial Relations Code, 2020, Act No. 35 of 2020 — Gazette of India, p. 24 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 24 · official download · all sources
