The Labour Codes in brief

The four Codes, the laws they replace, when they came into force, and whose rules apply to you.

Written from the official text · not reviewed by a lawyer · follows the Central Rules

Key points

  • The Labour Codes are the Code on Wages, 2019 s. 1(1), the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 s. 1(1), the Code on Social Security, 2020 s. 1(1) and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 s. 1(1).
  • They take the place of earlier laws. The Code on Wages repeals the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 and the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 s. 69(1).
  • The Industrial Relations Code repeals the Trade Unions Act, 1926, the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 s. 104(1).
  • The Code on Social Security repeals Acts including the Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948, the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 and the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 s. 164(1).
  • The OSH Code repeals Acts including the Factories Act, 1948, the Mines Act, 1952 and the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 s. 143(1).
  • The Central Government brought most of their provisions into force on 21 November 2025 S.O. 5322(E) S.O. 5320(E) S.O. 5319(E) S.O. 5321(E); each section’s page in this library shows its own status s. 1(3).
  • The appropriate Government is the Central Government for establishments of the Central Government, railways, mines, oil fields, major ports, air transport, telecommunication, banking and insurance companies and central public sector undertakings, and the State Government for any other establishment s. 2(d).
  • The appropriate Government makes the rules that fill in the detail of each Code s. 67(1); the Central Government’s rules under it are the Code on Wages (Central) Rules, 2026 rule 1.
  • Inspector-cum-Facilitators inspect establishments, and also advise employers and workers on complying with the Code s. 51(5).

If you are an employer

  • Find out first whether the Central or the State Government is your appropriate Government s. 2(d), because that Government makes the rules you follow s. 67(1) and appoints the Inspector-cum-Facilitators for your establishment s. 51(1).

If you are a worker

  • An Inspector-cum-Facilitator can advise you, as well as your employer, on what the Code requires s. 51(5)(a).

Check your understanding

7 questions. Choose an answer, then open “Show the answer” to check it against the law's own words.

  1. Question 1. Which Code repealed the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972?

    Show the answer

    Answer: The Code on Social Security, 2020

    The Code on Social Security, 2020 lists the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 among the enactments it repeals s. 164(1).

  2. Question 2. Which Code repealed the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947?

    Show the answer

    Answer: The Industrial Relations Code, 2020

    The Industrial Relations Code, 2020 repeals the Trade Unions Act, the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act and the Industrial Disputes Act s. 104(1).

  3. Question 3. From what date did the Central Government bring most provisions of the Code on Wages into force?

    Show the answer

    Answer: 21 November 2025

    S.O. 5322(E) appointed 21 November 2025 as the date on which most provisions of the Code on Wages came into force S.O. 5322(E).

  4. Question 4. Under the Code on Wages, which Government is the appropriate Government for a banking company?

    Show the answer

    Answer: The Central Government

    For banking and insurance companies, railways, mines, major ports and central public sector undertakings, among others, the appropriate Government is the Central Government s. 2(d)(i).

  5. Question 5. For a shop that is not in any of the Central Government's categories, which Government is the appropriate Government under the Code on Wages?

    Show the answer

    Answer: The State Government

    For any establishment outside the Central Government’s categories, the appropriate Government is the State Government s. 2(d)(ii).

  6. Question 6. Who makes the rules for carrying out the Code on Wages?

    Show the answer

    Answer: The appropriate Government

    The appropriate Government makes rules for carrying out the provisions of the Code, after previous publication s. 67(1).

  7. Question 7. Besides inspecting establishments, what may an Inspector-cum-Facilitator do under the Code on Wages?

    Show the answer

    Answer: Give advice to employers and workers on complying with the Code

    An Inspector-cum-Facilitator may advise employers and workers on complying with the Code, and inspect the establishments assigned to them s. 51(5).
